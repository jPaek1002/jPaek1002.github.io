Based on [Gaussian mixture layers for neural networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04883) by Professor Sinho Chewi. 
Essentially, instead of a finite number of neurons, we have a spectrum of neurons parameterized by a Gaussian mixture. 
Then we take the expected value of a neuron-esque operation over the Gaussian mixture. 
It is very important to note that empirically, this architecture is not amazing. 
However, it is an attempt to represent infinitely many parameters, which is cool.
My idea is not the most novel thing, but it works.
